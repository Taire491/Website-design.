# Elevate — Promotional brand film

**Campaign line:** Dream bigger. Build higher.

**Brand concept:** Elevate is a fictional personal-growth and creative-development platform. This short brand film uses ambition, learning, deliberate practice, and forward momentum as its visual story.

## Deliverables
- `Elevate_Brand_Film_30s.mp4` — 16:9 master, 30 seconds, 1280×720, 24 fps, original synthesized soundbed.
- `Elevate_Social_Cutdown_15s_9x16.mp4` — 9:16 vertical social version, 15 seconds. The full widescreen story is fitted into a vertical canvas to preserve all on-screen typography.
- `Elevate_Storyboard.pdf` — six-frame visual storyboard sheet.
- `assets/elevate-storyboard.jpg` — storyboard image.
- `assets/elevate-video-thumbnail.jpg` — campaign thumbnail.
- `assets/elevate_original_soundbed.wav` — original instrumental soundbed source.

## Production approach
- Original motion-graphic artwork built with vector-like shapes, layered gradients, typography, parallax-style movement, and animated progress motifs.
- Soundbed synthesized for this concept; it is not a licensed commercial music track.
- No external footage, client footage, testimonials, or performance claims are represented.
